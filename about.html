WHO WE ARE

A Learning Space Built For Students.

MathsWorld is designed to make learning resources easier to discover, access and use. Instead of searching through different places for study materials, students can find useful resources organized by grade, subject and year.

Our platform focuses on supporting students from Grade 6 to Grade 11 as well as Advanced Level students studying Mathematics, Biology, Physics and Chemistry.
